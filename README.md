An interactive Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, regions, and shipping performance.

🎯 Key KPIs
Total Orders
Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Customers
Number of Products
Sales & Profit by Month
Sales by Region
Sales by Segment
Sales & Profit by Ship Mode
Customer Distribution by Region, State & Subcategory
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel
Power Query – Data Cleaning & Transformation
Power Pivot – Data Modeling
DAX – Creating Measures & KPIs
PivotTables & PivotCharts – Data Analysis & Visualization
Interactive Slicers & Navigation – Dashboard Interaction
📊 Dashboard Pages
1. Overview

Provides a high-level summary of sales, profit, orders, customers, and products.

2. Sales Analysis

Analyzes sales performance across regions, months, quarters, segments, and shipping modes.

3. Customer Analysis

Provides insights into customer distribution by region, state, year, and product subcategory.

🎯 Objective

The goal of this project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive business dashboard that helps identify sales trends, profitable areas, customer distribution, and overall business performance.
